Beautiful Mountains of New Zealand

Rugged Ranges

New Zealand has many amazing mountain ranges. Popular skiing areas include Mount Hutt, Coronet Peak and Mount Ruapehu.

The climate in the mountains is often cold during winter with snow and strong winds.

In te reo Māori, mountain is called maunga.

Aoraki / Mount Cook

Aoraki / Mount Cook is the highest mountain in New Zealand. It is located in the South Island and is a famous tourist attraction.

Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park has 19 peaks over 3000 metres high.
